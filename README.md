# CrackingTheCodingInterview
This repo showcases the depth of my interview preparation for various jobs I have applied for. I have answered questions the 6th edition "Cracking the Coding Interview" suggested answering. There are technical explanations, purpose driven answers, and practiced coded solutions in various languages for a variety of problems. I have added relevant information and suggestions from other online sources to suppliment my learning goals based on current industry needs. All sites used during this process are listed to the best of my knowledge.
